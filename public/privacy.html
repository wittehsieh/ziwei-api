Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zi Wei Dou Shu Chart API ("the Service") processes information submitted through ziwei-api-henna.vercel.app.

The Service generates Zi Wei Dou Shu charts and related timing information from user-provided birth information.

1. Information We Process

When you use the Service, you may provide:

  • Birth date
  • Birth gender
  • Birth time
  • Birthplace or city
  • Country
  • Geographic coordinates, if provided
  • Time zone information, if provided
  • Calendar type and lunar-calendar information
  • Zi Wei Dou Shu hour index, if provided

The Service uses this information to calculate a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart, including natal chart information and related timing information such as Da Xian, Liu Nian, Liu Yue, and Liu Ri.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted to the Service is used to:

  • Generate a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart
  • Calculate true solar time when applicable
  • Calculate related timing information
  • Return the requested chart and calculation results to the requesting application

The Service does not use submitted birth information for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

The Service is designed to process submitted birth information for the purpose of generating the requested chart.

We do not intentionally create a personal profile or account based on the birth information submitted to the chart API.

However, requests may be processed through infrastructure and hosting providers that may maintain technical logs for security, reliability, abuse prevention, debugging, or operational purposes.

The exact retention period of such infrastructure logs may depend on the hosting provider and its applicable policies.

If the Service's data retention practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service may rely on third-party infrastructure or hosting providers to operate the API.

Such providers may process technical information necessary to deliver, secure, and maintain the Service.

We do not sell submitted birth information to third parties.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the Service and information transmitted to it.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for generating a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not specifically designed to collect information from children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the requested chart calculation.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to use the Service or not to submit your birth information.

Because the Service is primarily a chart-generation API and does not require a user account, there may be limited ability to identify or delete information from technical logs after a request has been processed.

If you have a privacy-related question or request, please contact the Service operator using the contact information provided with this Service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Service, data practices, or applicable requirements.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently revised.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by the Service, please contact the Service operator through the contact information associated with this project.
